# Brand Guidelines

## Brand

Product name: Fusionify Coffee

Fusionify Coffee belongs to the Fusionify ecosystem but should feel like a consumer coffee brand rather than a software dashboard.

## Confirmed Family Colors

- Fusionify Primary: `#0D6CD7`
- Fusionify Deep: `#0261CC`
- Fusionify Supporting: `#539DE9`

Use blue as identity, not as every background/surface.

## Coffee Personality

Coffee warmth should come primarily from:
- Product photography
- Warm neutral surfaces
- Natural copy
- Material/product imagery
- Restrained accent use

Do not turn the entire brand brown solely because it sells coffee.

Any permanent coffee-specific accent palette remains provisional until explicitly approved.

## Logo Rules

Until official Fusionify Coffee logo assets are committed:
- Do not invent an official logo
- Do not generate replacement brand marks
- Do not declare a temporary AI-generated logo as official

When official assets are provided:
- Preserve geometry
- Preserve approved colors
- Do not stretch
- Do not add gradients
- Do not add effects without approval
- Keep symbol/wordmark variants documented

### Provisional Fusion F-Bean Concept

The current development build may use the clearly labeled `fusion-f-bean` assets for app-icon and splash evaluation. It is a flat, solid-color design exploration commissioned for this preview, not the approved official Fusionify Coffee logo.

Do not present it as final in marketing, store listings, or production releases. Once identity approval is complete, replace the source master and regenerate platform icon sizes from that approved source.

## Tone

Customer copy should be:
- Short
- Clear
- Friendly
- Natural
- Action-oriented

Avoid generic AI copy such as:
- "Elevate your coffee experience"
- "Unlock a world of delightful rewards"
- "Seamlessly discover your perfect brew"

Prefer direct product language such as:
- "Pesananmu siap diambil."
- "Pilih outlet untuk pickup."
- "Pembayaran belum diterima."

## Competitors

Fore Coffee, Kopi Kenangan, Starbucks, and other established apps may be studied for usability patterns.

Do not copy their:
- Logos
- Artwork
- Screens
- Copy
- Exact layout
- Proprietary assets

Premium payment-card products may be studied only for broad material cues such as restraint, hierarchy, and perceived quality. Fusionify membership artwork must not reproduce issuer logos, network marks, portraits, card details, or distinctive trade dress.
