# ADR 0005: Use a No-Gradient Product Design Language

Date: 2026-08-28
Status: Accepted

## Context

Fusionify Coffee needs a consistent visual identity that avoids generic AI-generated visual styling and relies on product imagery, hierarchy, solid color, spacing, and typography.

## Decision

Decorative gradients are prohibited in Fusionify Coffee product UI.

This includes linear, radial, and sweep gradients.

## Consequences

Design must create depth and hierarchy using:
- Solid brand/surface colors
- Product photography
- Typography
- Whitespace
- Borders
- Restrained elevation
- Purposeful motion

Any future decision to introduce gradients requires a new accepted design decision and update to the design system.

## Alternatives Considered

Using gradients selectively was rejected for the initial design language to maintain a clear and enforceable visual constraint.
