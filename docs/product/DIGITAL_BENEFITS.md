# Digital Benefits

Status: future feature.

Fusionify Coffee may connect physical purchases with digital benefits.

## Planned Concepts

- Digital receipt
- Time-limited outlet Wi-Fi entitlement
- AI access/quota entitlement
- Membership-based benefit differences

## Principle

Benefits are entitlements tied to verified business events, not secrets embedded directly into receipts or mobile code.

## Wi-Fi

Future design should consider:
- Outlet-specific access
- Expiry
- Secure reveal
- Abuse prevention
- Retry/recovery
- Network-device integration

## AI Access

Future design should consider:
- Gateway-controlled access
- Quota
- Expiry/reset
- Revocation
- Secure token material
- Cost control
- Abuse prevention

## Privacy and Security

Do not expose long-lived infrastructure credentials in a receipt.

Do not implement digital benefits until the order/payment entitlement model is trustworthy.
